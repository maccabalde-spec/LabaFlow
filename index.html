Got Questions?
Frequently Asked Questions
How does the RFID tracking work? +
When you drop off your laundry, our staff assigns a unique RFID tag to your bag. The tag is scanned at every step — Received, Processing, Ready, and Released. You can log in to your LabaFlow account anytime to see your order's exact status.
What detergents and products do you use? +
We use Surf Liquid Detergent (0.1 L per load) for cleaning, Surf Fabric Conditioner (0.1 L per load) for softness and scent, and Zonrox Color Safe Bleach (10 ml per load) to remove bacteria without damaging colors. These products are auto-dosed per load.
How is the price calculated? +
Clothes: ₱180 base for 7 kg, +₱25 per extra kg. 10 kg and above is split into 2 sets of max 9 kg each. Bedsheets: ₱190 base for 5 kg, +₱38 per extra kg. Comforters: flat ₱200 per piece regardless of weight. Our system auto-calculates when you book.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept Cash and GCash. Payment is recorded when you pick up your laundry. Paying for your Ready order automatically marks it as Released and frees up your RFID tag.
What are LabaPoints and how do they work? +
LabaPoints are our loyalty rewards. Every ₱1 spent earns 1 LabaPoint. Reach 500 points for Silver tier (5% discount), 1,000 for Gold tier (10% discount), and 2,000 for Platinum tier (15% discount). Points can be redeemed for discounts on future orders.
Can I track my order as a customer? +
Yes! Register a customer account and you can view your order status in real-time, book new services online, message our staff directly, and see your full order history — all from your LabaFlow portal.
How long does washing take? +
Wash time is about 2–3 hours, depending on how many loads are in the queue when you drop off. You will get a "Ready" alert in your LabaFlow account as soon as your laundry is done.
Can I mix whites and colored clothes? +
When booking, you choose Whites Only, Colored Only, or Mixed. If you choose Mixed, Lab-a-Day Laundry is not liable for color bleeding or color transfer between clothes, so we recommend separating whites and colored.
Where is the shop and how do I contact you? +
Lab-a-Day Laundry is at B. Cosme Street, Poblacion 2, Pagsanjan, Laguna. Call or text us at 0961 130 8218.
Do you accept special instructions? +
Absolutely. When booking, there's a Special Instructions field where you can specify requests like "separate whites," "no bleach," "extra rinse," etc. Staff will see these notes when processing your order.
