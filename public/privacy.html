LabaFlow Privacy Policy

Lab-a-Day Laundry Operations, RFID Tracking & Customer Portal

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lab-a-Day Laundry ("we", "our", or "the shop") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the LabaFlow Android application (com.labaday.labaflow) and companion web portal.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone/contact number, and account role provided during registration or order booking.
  • Laundry Order & RFID Records: Laundry service type, weight/loads, RFID bag tag status, queue status, LabaPoints loyalty balance, and customer-shop messages.
  • Uploaded Payment Proofs & Images: Images you choose to upload (such as GCash payment confirmation screenshots or store/profile photos) via the Android file picker.
  • Device & Notification Permissions: Network connectivity state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), vibration (VIBRATE), and optional push notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) used strictly to alert you about order updates and verification codes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your LabaFlow customer or staff account.
  • Provide real-time RFID laundry tracking from drop-off through washing, drying, folding, and pickup.
  • Verify Cash/GCash payments and credit LabaPoints loyalty rewards.
  • Deliver order alerts, OTP verification codes, and support replies.

3. Data Storage & Security

LabaFlow transmits application data over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections and stores records securely using Supabase cloud database and authentication services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

We retain order and account records only as long as necessary to operate Lab-a-Day Laundry services and fulfill customer support or accounting obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.
